1. Information We Collect

Information you give us when you sign in

You sign in with an email address and password, or with Google or Apple. If you use a provider, we receive from them your unique account identifier, your email address, and, where available, your display name and profile picture. If you use Sign in with Apple and choose to hide your email, we receive a private relay address instead of your real one.

If you register with an email address and password, your password is handled entirely by Google Firebase Authentication, our authentication provider. It is transmitted over an encrypted connection and stored by them only as a salted cryptographic hash. Neither we nor anyone operating BookTalks can see, retrieve, or recover your password, and it is never written to our database, our logs, or any analytics service. We ask you to confirm your email address before using the Service so that we know the address belongs to you and can reach you about your account.

Profile information you provide

  • Username, biography, and profile picture (including its shape and crop).
  • Date of birth. We use it to verify that you meet our minimum age requirement of 17, and, on the day itself, to let the friends you have added know that it is your birthday. Your full date of birth is never shown to anyone.
  • Favorite genres.
  • Your privacy preferences.

Location (optional)

If you grant location permission, we collect your approximate device location (accurate to roughly one kilometre) and use Apple’s geocoding service to convert it into a city, state, and country. We store your coordinates together with that city, state, and country on your profile, and refresh them at most once a day. Location is used to help you find nearby bookstores and libraries and to suggest readers near you.

Location is entirely optional. If you revoke permission in iOS Settings, we delete your stored coordinates, city, state, and country from your profile.

Camera (optional) — barcode scanning

If you grant camera permission, you can scan the barcode on the back of a book to add it to one of your lists. The camera view we use recognises barcodes only: it has no shutter and cannot take a photograph. No image, video, or frame is ever captured, written to your device, or transmitted to us or anyone else.

The only thing produced by a scan is the book’s ISBN — the number printed under the barcode. We look that number up the same way we look up any book you search for: first in our own book cache, and if it isn’t there, with our book data providers. We do not record that you scanned it, where you scanned it, or when.

Camera access is entirely optional and is only ever used while the scanner is open. You can revoke it at any time in iOS Settings, or from Settings inside the app, and every other part of BookTalks continues to work.

Advertising

BookTalks is supported by advertising. We show a single full-screen advertisement when you open or return to the app after signing in — nowhere else in the app carries ads. Ads are served by Google AdMob, which acts as an independent controller of the data it collects for this purpose.

To serve and measure those ads, Google may collect your device’s advertising identifier, IP address, general device information, an approximate location derived from your IP address, and how you interacted with the ad. We do not send Google your name, email address, reading lists, messages, or the precise location you may have shared with us for the nearby-readers feature.

iOS asks your permission before any app may use the advertising identifier for tracking. If you allow it, the ads you see may be personalised using that identifier. If you decline, you will still see ads, but they will be non-personalised — chosen without using that identifier to build a profile of you. Declining changes nothing else about the app.

  • Change your choice at any time: iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Google explains its own handling of advertising data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.
  • If you are in the EEA or UK, we ask for your consent before personalised ads are served, and you can change that choice from within the app.

You may also choose to watch an advertisement voluntarily, from the Support screen, as a way of supporting the app. That is entirely optional, gives you nothing beyond our thanks and a count of how many you have watched, and the same handling described above applies.

We never sell your personal information, and we do not share your reading activity, messages, or profile content with advertisers.

Security and sign-in records

When you open BookTalks, we record the time, your IP address, your device model, your iOS version and your app version. This is kept for one purpose: so that a suspicious sign-in can be investigated if you or we believe your account has been accessed by someone else. It is the same information a bank or email provider keeps for the same reason.

This is NOT the optional location feature described above, and the two are kept separate. We do not store a location for your sign-ins, we do not use any of this to personalise what you see, and we never sell or share it. If we need to check roughly where a sign-in came from during an investigation, we look the IP address up at that moment rather than keeping a record of your movements.

Sign-in records are automatically deleted after 90 days.

Donations — what we do not collect

If you choose to make a donation, it is processed entirely by Apple. We never receive, see, or store your card number, billing address, or any other payment detail — that information passes from you to Apple and never reaches the app or our servers.

We also keep no record of the donation itself. Nothing is written to your profile or our database, no donation is linked to your account, and we cannot tell from within the app whether any particular person has donated. Apple provides us only with aggregate sales figures, which do not identify you.

Content you create

We store the content you create in the app: reading lists, reading progress, book reviews and ratings, private notes and highlights, your profile canvas and any background image you upload, posts, polls, poll votes, comments, likes, direct and group messages (including images and edit history), reviews of bookstores and libraries, support requests, and reports you submit.

Information collected automatically

  • Device and app information such as device model, operating-system version, app version, and language, collected by Google Firebase Analytics.
  • Basic usage events such as app opens and screen views. We do not log custom analytics events about what you read or post.
  • An approximate region derived from your IP address by our infrastructure providers.
  • A push-notification token for your device, if you enable notifications.

We do not sell your personal information. Advertising in BookTalks is described in the Advertising section above: where you permit tracking, Google may use your device’s advertising identifier to personalise the advertisements you see. We do not share your reading activity, lists, notes, or messages with advertisers, and we do not use those for advertising purposes.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and operate your account and provide the features you use.
  • To display your profile and content to the audiences you choose.
  • To connect you with friends and deliver messages and notifications.
  • To personalize book recommendations and nearby suggestions.
  • To verify that you meet our minimum age requirement.
  • To keep the Service safe — investigating reports, moderating content, detecting abuse, spam, and fraud, and enforcing our Terms.
  • To provide support and respond to your requests.
  • To understand overall usage and improve the app.
  • To comply with legal obligations and to establish, exercise, or defend legal claims.

We do not use your private notes, messages, or reading history to train machine-learning or artificial-intelligence models, and we do not sell your personal information.

3. Who Can See Your Information

Other users

Your username, profile picture, biography, and profile canvas are visible to other users, subject to your privacy settings. Reviews, public posts, polls, comments, and place reviews are attributed to you. Poll votes and likes are recorded against your account and are not anonymous, though other users see only totals. Private notes, private lists, and direct messages are not public.

Privacy settings let you make your account private, restrict who can see your lists and friends, restrict who can interact with your canvas, and hide yourself from search.

Two reading lists, Private Want to Read and Private Already Read, are never visible to anyone but you. That applies regardless of your other privacy settings, and it does not change when your account is public. Books you place on those lists are not used in recommendations shown to other people and are not included in anything you share.

Service providers

We share information with providers who process it on our behalf, under contract and only for the purposes we specify:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore database, Cloud Storage, Cloud Messaging, Analytics) — hosting, storage, and delivery of the Service.
  • Google Sign-In and Apple (Sign in with Apple) — authentication.
  • Apple (Maps and geocoding) — converting coordinates into place names and finding nearby places.
  • Google AdMob — serving and measuring the advertisement shown when you open the app.

Searches you run are sent to the third-party catalogues that answer them — Google Books, ISBNdb, Open Library, AniList, Gutendex/Project Gutenberg, and Archive of Our Own — so that they can return results. Best-seller data from The New York Times is fetched by our servers on a schedule and is not tied to you: your searches are never sent to them. We do not send them your identity.

Legal and safety

We may disclose information where we believe in good faith it is necessary to comply with a law, regulation, legal process, or governmental request; to enforce our Terms; to detect or address fraud, security, or technical issues; or to protect the rights, property, or safety of our users, the public, or us. We report suspected child sexual abuse material to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children and to law enforcement as required by law.

Business transfers

If the Service is involved in a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, your information may be transferred. We will give notice before your information becomes subject to a materially different privacy policy.

4. How Long We Keep Information

  • Account and profile data: while your account is active.
  • Deleted account: deactivated immediately and permanently deleted after a 30-day grace period, during which you can restore it by signing back in.
  • Individually deleted content (messages, reviews, comments, place reviews): held in a restricted archive for up to 30 days for accidental-deletion recovery and abuse investigations, then permanently deleted.
  • Feed activity items: automatically expire after 60 days.
  • Downloaded book and fanfiction text: cached only on your device and deleted when you sign out.
  • Reports and moderation records: retained as long as needed for safety and enforcement purposes.
  • Information we must keep by law: retained for the required period.

5. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to our use of your personal information, and to receive a copy of it in a portable format. You can exercise many of these directly in the app:

  • Access and correct: Settings → Edit Profile.
  • Control visibility: Settings → Account Privacy.
  • Export: your book reviews can be exported as PDF files from any review.
  • Delete: Settings → Delete Account.
  • Location: revoke permission in iOS Settings, which also deletes stored location data.
  • Notifications: turn off in iOS Settings, or mute individual conversations in the app.

For anything you cannot do in the app, contact info.booktalks@gmail.com and we will respond within the period required by applicable law. We will not discriminate against you for exercising these rights.

California residents

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act, as amended, California residents have the right to know what personal information we collect, use, and disclose; to request deletion or correction; and to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information. We do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined, and we do not use sensitive personal information for purposes requiring an opt-out. You may exercise your rights as described above, and you may designate an authorised agent to act for you.

Users in the European Economic Area and United Kingdom

Where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies, our legal bases for processing are: performance of our contract with you (operating your account and the features you use); your consent (location and push notifications, which you can withdraw at any time); our legitimate interests (keeping the Service safe and secure, preventing abuse, and improving the app); and compliance with legal obligations. You may lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority.

6. International Transfers

If you use the Service from outside the United States, you understand that your information will be transferred to and processed in the United States, where data-protection laws may differ from those in your country. 7. Security

We use industry-standard measures to protect your information, including encryption in transit, authenticated access, and server-side rules that restrict who can read and write each piece of data. Direct messages are stored on our infrastructure and are not end-to-end encrypted.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. Please protect your device and sign-in credentials, and tell us at info.booktalks@gmail.com if you suspect unauthorised access to your account.

8. Children’s Privacy

BookTalks is not intended for anyone under 17, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under that age. If we learn that we have collected information from someone under 17, we will delete the account and the information promptly.

If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact info.booktalks@gmail.com so we can remove it.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we make a material change, we will update the version and effective date above and ask you to review and accept the updated version in the app. Continuing to use the Service after an update takes effect means you accept it.
